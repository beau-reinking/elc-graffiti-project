# Initial open test operations

## Tester note

GT Graffiti is an experimental research project for adults 18+. Use current desktop Chrome or Firefox on Windows or macOS. Mobile, Safari and other browsers are experimental. Keep the tab open while clips prepare; large recordings may take several minutes and consume memory. Keep your own recording copies.

Graffiti handles accounts. Localhost browser drafts and sessions do not automatically appear on the public site. Published profiles, recordings and approved events are public through Graffiti and may be readable outside these apps. An excerpt does not publish the full original. Restricted conversations are not described as end-to-end encrypted. Blocking hides interactions in this app; it cannot prevent access to public data elsewhere. App-data removal does not delete the Graffiti identity or others' copies.

For bugs, send the copied diagnostic summary, what you tried and browser/OS to **breinking3@gatech.edu**. Do not email credentials, private recordings, messages or full account identifiers. No automatic analytics or third-party error telemetry is enabled.

## Reviewer routine

Sign in using the configured project-reviewer actor. In Yudo Profile → Review reports, review reports daily during the initial test. Confirm report delivery before inviting people. Apply app-level exclusions where appropriate; only authors can delete their Graffiti objects. Reply to support requests manually when appropriate. Do not claim universal takedown powers.

In OurScene, open the Event review inbox. Check title, dates/timezone, venue/address, event links and permission to share supplied material. Submissions are not a legal ownership verification. Approve accurate upcoming events, reject others, and withdraw inaccurate/expired/abusive approved listings. Approval publishes a sanitized snapshot authored by the reviewer; it does not impersonate a venue. Pending submissions stay restricted to creator and reviewer; private source object URLs are not included in the public decision. Withdrawal creates a public terminal marker so a later stale approval cannot reactivate that submission.

Check support mail, report queue, event queue, provider storage/usage policies and Pages build quota daily during week one. Record manual operational notes without message or recording contents. If a free provider becomes insufficient, pause the affected release path and decide separately before enabling paid infrastructure. No background push or email delivery service is configured.
