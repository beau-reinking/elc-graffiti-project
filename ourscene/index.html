Try the complete flow

  1. Load demo events, then open OurScene. The map starts with matching event locations.
  2. Select a numbered pin to see event thumbnails; choose one to open its details.
  3. Try dates, maximum price, Free only and Genres. Map and List share the filters.
  4. Open a venue’s Community tab. Visitor demo handles: river_notes, milo_live, sky_sounds. Post, tag an event, and reply.
  5. Use Venue owner sign in with its test handle, then Owner area to manage events. Owner-only community controls hide and restore threads.

All venues and map sites are fictional demonstrations. Ticket links are labeled demos, not real purchase offers. Local identities are insecure test identities; data stays in this browser and origin. The map basemap needs an internet connection. Deployed apps use decentralized Graffiti and real venue actors.

Existing Sprint 1 announcements remain available in the legacy section; no dates are invented for them.
