Sylt · Privacy Policy

User Agreement

Sylt is an experimental research project. By using a Sylt account, you agree to these terms and confirm that you are at least 18 years old. Use a Graffiti identity you control and follow your provider’s terms.

Your music and recordings

You retain ownership of your content and give Sylt and its providers permission to store and deliver it for the features and audiences you choose. Upload and share material you have permission to use, including permission from people you record. Public shares can be copied and forwarded.

How to participate

Respect other members. Harassment, threats, impersonation, spam, unlawful content, rights violations, and attempts to bypass access controls are prohibited. Use blocking or hiding to manage what you see. Contact the project about abuse or rights concerns.

Privacy, feedback, and availability

The Privacy Policy describes data handling and deletion. Feedback is optional. This agreement does not enroll you in a separate research study. Features and provider availability may change. Keep a copy of any work you want to preserve. You can stop using Sylt at any time and remove recordings using the app.
