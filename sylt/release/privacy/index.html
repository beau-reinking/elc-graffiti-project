Jive · User Agreement

Privacy Policy

Jive is an experimental research project for adults aged 18 or older. Questions, feedback, and privacy requests go to breinking3@gatech.edu.

What Jive uses

Your Graffiti account identifier connects your profile, uploads, friendships, comments, and settings. Profiles are public through Graffiti. Your browser asks for camera or microphone access when you choose to record. Your take stays in the browser until you choose to save it.

Who can access recordings

Saved practice takes are private by default. Sharing with friends creates a separate copy available to the audience selected at the time of sharing. Removing a friend leaves earlier shares with their original audience. Creating a public link makes a separate copy public through Graffiti, where other people or clients may find or copy it.

Where information goes

Graffiti providers handle sign-in and store and deliver records and media. Jive does not collect Graffiti passwords or passkeys. Cloudflare delivers the site. An external avatar host may receive a request from your browser when its image loads. Website delivery, sign-in, and storage providers may process network information such as your IP address under their own policies. Jive does not include advertising or product analytics. Demo recordings and browser session information stay in storage specific to this site.

Retention and removal

Saved data stays with its Graffiti provider until removed. Use My Practice to delete recordings and remove individual shares. Deleting an original also removes Jive shares and linked public excerpts created from it. Deletion cannot retract copies others have downloaded or records authored by other people. Clearing your browser storage removes its local data. Contact us for privacy requests and help with removal. Feedback you email is used to respond and improve the project.
