Interface annotations

  1. A single social home: The feed and friend management share Home. The Friends control opens a desktop drawer or full-screen mobile panel without navigating away or losing the feed position.
  2. One clear primary action: Log practice remains available in the desktop rail and the mobile bottom navigation. The home invitation reinforces this action without daily goals or streak pressure.
  3. Private completion first: A successfully saved take earns its own warm completion state. Sharing is a separate decision, with friends and public links explicitly distinguished.
  4. People before performance: Chronological posts, manual playback, plain-text encouragement, and small musician profiles support relatedness without popularity ranking.
  5. Personal progress: My Practice is an accessible archive of earlier recordings. Audio and video filters aid retrieval without grading musicians.
  6. Responsive hierarchy: Narrow screens retain the feed and primary action; secondary home cards disappear. Friend management expands to fill the screen.
