Privacy Policy

Version 1.0 · Prepared November 6, 2026. Effective when this web release becomes available.

GT Graffiti is an experimental research project bringing together Yudo, Sylt, and OurScene. Questions, feedback, and privacy inquiries go to breinking3@gatech.edu.

Information the apps use

The apps process your Graffiti account identifier and the information you supply: musician profiles, approximate location, musical preferences, recordings, connection preferences, likes, passes, friendships, messages, community posts, and reports. Graffiti manages authentication. The apps receive a session and account identifier, not your passkey’s secret.

Public and restricted information

Published profiles, public recordings and excerpts, venue profiles, events, and community posts may be accessible through Graffiti outside this website. Other people may copy them. Drafts, private recordings, settings, and interactions use the access lists explained in the apps. Friends-only shares retain their originally selected audience until the share is removed. Restricted access is not a promise of end-to-end encryption.

Location, camera, and microphone

Yudo publishes a city label and approximate location. If you permit device location access, it derives an approximate location and discards precise coordinates. Real venue addresses and event locations may be public. Camera and microphone access is requested when you choose recording features. You can revoke permissions in your browser.

Preparing excerpts

On the hosted website, clips are processed in your browser. Source media is retrieved from its Graffiti provider; there is no additional processing-server upload. Prepared clips are saved privately until you explicitly publish. Publishing a selected excerpt does not make the original recording public. The optional native launcher processes clips with its local media service.

How information is used

Information operates profiles, discovery, connections, messages, recordings, event discussions, moderation, support, and security. Reviewers receive the information you include in reports. They do not automatically receive access to all your private messages or recordings.

Event submissions

Anyone signed in may submit an event. Pending details and review decisions are restricted to the submitter and project reviewer. After explicit submission consent and reviewer approval, the approved event details and submission identity may be public. Reviewer approval is not venue ownership verification. Contact the project to request correction or removal of approved details.

Research and feedback

This release does not automatically track you for research or analyze your profiles, messages, or recordings for research. Feedback is voluntary. Correspondence you send may be used to respond and improve the apps. Future interviews, surveys, or additional research collection will be explained separately before participation or collection.

Service providers and browser storage

Graffiti providers store and deliver app records and media. Cloudflare serves the website. Maps, externally hosted images, links, and email providers may receive information needed to provide their services, including IP addresses and request details. Their policies and retention practices apply. See Cloudflare’s privacy policy and the policies of your selected Graffiti provider. Your browser stores sessions, preferences, drafts, demo data, and static encoder assets. We do not enable advertising trackers, automatic research analytics, or external error telemetry, and do not sell personal information.

Retention, editing, and removal

Graffiti records generally remain with their providers until removed through the relevant app or provider. Browser information remains until removed by the app or cleared in browser settings. Feedback is retained as needed to respond and evaluate issues. You may edit or remove your authored content and request help with access, correction, or deletion by email; verifying your account ownership may be necessary.

App-data removal does not delete your Graffiti identity, other people’s records, downloaded copies, or separate feedback emails. Removing Yudo data does not delete original Sylt recordings. Provider logs and backups follow provider practices. Incomplete deletion is reported for retry. The project cannot universally erase another actor’s Graffiti data.

Security, adults, and changes

HTTPS and Graffiti access restrictions protect information, but no online service guarantees complete security. Contact us about suspected exposure. These apps are intended for adults aged 18 or older; contact us if a child has supplied information. Material changes will be announced in the apps before a changed data-use practice begins.
