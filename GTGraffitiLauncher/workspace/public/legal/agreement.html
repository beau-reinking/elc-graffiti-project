User Agreement

Version 1.0 · Prepared November 6, 2026. Effective when this web release becomes available.

This agreement covers Yudo, Sylt, and OurScene in the GT Graffiti experimental research project. Questions and feedback go to breinking3@gatech.edu.

Your account

Account-based features are for people aged 18 or older. Use only accounts you are authorized to control and protect your Graffiti credentials. Graffiti providers have their own authentication processes and terms. By choosing to use account-based features, you agree to these rules. You may browse the legal pages or stop using the apps at any time.

Your content

You retain ownership of your content. You permit the project and its providers to store, process, and display it as needed to operate the features and audiences you select. Share only material you have the right to share, including necessary permissions for recordings and other performers. Public access does not transfer ownership of your work.

Published material may be accessible through other Graffiti clients and may be copied. Preparing an excerpt is separate from publishing it. A public Sylt excerpt does not change the original recording’s audience.

Acceptable use

Do not harass, threaten, discriminate, impersonate others, commit fraud, spam, exploit people, share unlawful material or private information without authorization, or infringe others’ rights. Do not bypass access controls, misuse credentials, disrupt services, or access someone else’s restricted information. Only authorized venue accounts may use venue-owner tools.

Event submissions

You may submit accurate upcoming event information that you have permission to share. Submissions remain restricted until approved. The project reviewer may approve, reject, or withdraw a listing. Approval does not make you a venue owner or verify the event. Do not imply a venue endorses this project. Request corrections or removal through the project contact.

Connections, events, and moderation

Use appropriate judgment when arranging collaborations or meetings. Profiles, matches, listings, and event information are not guarantees of identity, business ownership, availability, employment, or successful collaboration. Report abuse through the apps or project contact.

The project may restrict access or hide content within these apps to address abuse, security issues, or violations. Venue owners may moderate their communities. These measures do not authorize deletion of another actor’s Graffiti objects. Blocking and unmatching do not retract received copies or prevent access to public content through other clients.

Privacy and research

The Privacy Policy explains data handling. Feedback is optional. This agreement does not authorize additional research collection or enroll you in a separate study. Any future research participation will be described separately.

Experimental service and leaving

Features may change, fail, or become unavailable. Keep your own copies of recordings you want to preserve. You may leave at any time and use available tools to remove authored content and app data. Deletion has the limits explained in the Privacy Policy, including copies, other people’s records, and independently hosted data.

To the extent permitted by applicable law, the experimental service is provided without guarantees of uninterrupted operation or suitability for a particular purpose. Nothing here removes rights that cannot legally be waived. Graffiti providers, maps, hosting services, and external sites have their own terms and practices.

Changes and contact

Material agreement changes will be announced before further account-based use. Questions, complaints, privacy concerns, and content-rights inquiries may be sent to breinking3@gatech.edu.
